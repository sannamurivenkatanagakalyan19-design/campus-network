iMONITORING MODEL These concepts explain how campus login activity is represented, evaluated, and surfaced for review.

HOW THE MONITORING WORKS

From identity to investigation

01 / DESIGN

Object-oriented modeling

Accounts, devices, login events, and alerts are modeled as distinct domain objects. Student and staff roles provide context for interpreting an event.

02 / ADSA

Relationship graphs

A login connects an account to a device, location, and event. Graph views make those relationships and repeated patterns easier to inspect.

03 / DMGT

Propositional logic

Rules combine evidence with operators such as AND and OR. For example, a new device used at an unusual hour can trigger a suspicious-login review.

04 / ANALYSIS

Anomaly detection

Statistical analysis compares login frequency with expected patterns and flags unusual activity for investigation, rather than treating it as proof of misuse.
